If You Buy Your Girlfriend a Country Concert Ticket...

If you buy your girlfriend a country concert ticket, she's going to want to wear a cute outfit.

If she wears a cute outfit, she’s going to want you to take pictures of her

If you take pictures of her, she’s going to want a drink.

If she has a drink, she’s going to ask you to take her to another country concert.
