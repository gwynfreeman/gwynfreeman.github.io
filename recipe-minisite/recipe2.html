Peanut Butter Cookies

Yield: 22 cookies

Time: 22 minutes

Ingredients

  • ½ cup butter
  • ½ cup peanut butter
  • ½ cup suagar
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 ¼ cups sifted flour
  • ⅔ teaspoon baking soda
  • ¼ teaspoon salt

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375° F.
  2. Mix first six ingredients.
  3. Add the rest of the ingredients.
  4. Mix well.
  5. Roll into balls and press down with a fork dipped in sugar.
  6. Bake for 10-12 minutes on ungreased cookie sheet.
