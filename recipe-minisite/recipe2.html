Peanut Butter Cookies

Yield: 22 cookies

Time: 22 minutes

Ingredients

  • ½ cup butter
  • ½ cup peanut butter
  • ½ cup suagar
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 ¼ cups sifted flour
  • ⅔ teaspoon baking soda
  • ¼ teaspoon salt

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350° F.
  2. Line 2 large baking sheets with parchment paper.
  3. Whisk together flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt.
  4. Whisk melted butter, brown sugar, and sugar until paste-like.
  5. Whisk in egg and vanilla.
  6. Fold in dry ingredients, then chocolate chips.
  7. Scoop 6 cookies per sheet and bake 10–12 minutes.
  8. Look for golden edges and pale centers.
  9. Top with extra chocolate chips + sea salt.
  10. Cool on a rack and bake remaining dough.
  11. Let cool 20 minutes before eating.
