Plant Name

Rhododendron macrophyllum

Common Alternate Names

Pacific Rhododendron, California Rhododendron

Image of Plant

Range of Physical Size for the Plant

They can grow between 5-25 feet in height and as such they can be grown as a shrub or a small tree. Their size is dependent on how much sunlight they receive and how they are maintained.

Range of Geography Where the Plant can be Found

This plant grows from British Columbia to California. The plant grows west of the Cascades crest and at the coast in Washington.

The types of climate where the plant grows best

It grows in fairly dry open forests and edges. It commonly grows along roadsides in the rainshadow of the Olympic Mountains. Pacific rhododendrons prefer well-draining, acidic soil with a low nitrogen content.
